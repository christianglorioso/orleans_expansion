# Pokémon Orleans

**Pokémon Orleans** is a GBA ROM hack set in **Orleans**, a region inspired by Louisiana: bayous and cypress swamps,
a Mississippi-style river, levees and storm country, a jazz-age city on the crescent of the river, sugar-cane and pepper
fields, salt domes and the open Gulf. It is built on [RHH's `pokeemerald-expansion`](https://github.com/rh-hideout/pokeemerald-expansion).

> Work in progress. The ROM builds as `pokemon_orleans.gba`.

## The game

- **A new region**: 11 towns and cities and 25 routes, drawn tile by tile for this project, with organic edges and
  seamless joins between maps.
- **A full story**: eight Singers (gyms) and the Telling, eight verses that tell the story of the region; the rival Jayden;
  Wren of the Old Water; Team Blacksnake and its leader Andrew; and a finale with Kyogre, Groudon and Rayquaza.
- **The Orleans Pokédex**: a regional dex of 300 species with Orleanian regional forms, retyped and rebalanced
  Pokémon, new Mega Evolutions, custom abilities and learnsets. Trade evolutions use the Linking Cord.
- **A post-game**: catchable legendaries in their own lairs, the Battle Frontier district in Crescent City, rematch
  raids, and every evolution item and Mega Stone obtainable.
- **Quality of life**:
  - an in-game clock (a full day every 72 minutes of play) with rest spots at every healer;
  - overworld and battle speed options;
  - DexNav;
  - type and effectiveness display in battle;
  - move relearning from the summary screen;
  - nature and gender shown for starters and wild encounters, so they can be soft reset;
  - EX vitamins and Game Corner minigames.

## Building

Follow [`INSTALL.md`](INSTALL.md) to set up the toolchain, then build as for `pokeemerald-expansion` (`make`). The
Orleans maps, tilesets and story scripts are generated by the tools in [`tools/mapgen/`](tools/mapgen/README.md), and a
headless playtest bot lives in [`tools/playtest/`](tools/playtest/README.md).
