# Tileset credits for the composed Orleans tilesets

Generated tilesets under `data/tilesets/*/orleans_*` are built by `tools/mapgen/compose.py` from the
sources below. Keep this list in the game's credits.

## gTileset_OrleansGeneral (primary), gTileset_OrleansBayou (secondary, HnS parts)
From the Pokémon Heart & Soul project (https://github.com/PokemonHnS-Development/pokemonHnS), whose
README credits these tileset artists: Crystal Advance (Kertra), Ekat99, TheDeadHeroAlistair,
Johto Redrawn Team.
Recoloured to the Orleans swamp palette.

## gTileset_OrleansBayou: swamp pieces
"Swamp Tiles" by Ekat99 — https://www.deviantart.com/ekat99/art/Swamp-Tiles-878136634
Public use for non-commercial projects. Credit: Ekat, Heartlessdragoon (RSE rips).
Source sheet: `tools/mapgen/sources/ekat_swamp/swamp_tiles.png`.

## gTileset_OrleansCity
"Brick City" by Ekat99 — https://www.deviantart.com/ekat99/art/Brick-City-895740356
pokeemerald import by Kumatora (Team Aqua's Asset Repo,
https://github.com/TeamAquasHideout/Team-Aquas-Asset-Repo).
Public use for non-commercial projects. Credit: Ekat, Vurtax (FRLG rips), Heartlessdragoon (RSE rips),
Alistar (tree bases and short grass patches), Slimshady (mailboxes); Kumatora for the import.
Source files: `tools/mapgen/sources/brick_city/`.

## gTileset_OrleansBayou: houses, barn, headstones, levee
- Houses: Shady Forest secondary (Team Aqua's Asset Repo), assembled by Yumekua, insertable reformat
  by Rahtak; main creators Ekat99, Heartlessdragoon, Vurtax; full list in
  `tools/mapgen/sources/shady_forest/credits.md`. Recoloured off its teal ground.
- Dance hall: Moomoo Farm barn from the HnS Route 38 farmland tileset (HnS credits above).
- Pier: Olivine / Route 40 dock from the HnS Route 40 tileset (HnS credits above).
- Headstones: HnS Soul House tileset, recoloured to grey stone (HnS credits above).
- Church and sailboats: Brick City (credits below).
- Levee ridge: Lavaridge tileset from Pokémon Emerald (pret/pokeemerald), recoloured to mud brown.

## Original art

- Capsized pirogue (`sources/orleans_custom/pirogue.png`): drawn for this project by `make_pirogue.py`. No outside source.
